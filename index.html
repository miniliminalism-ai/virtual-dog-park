Dog Encyclopedia

Three breeds to start with. Each card shows a picture, the size, the personality, and the everyday care the dog needs.

Beagle

Size
Small. About 33–41 cm tall and 9–11 kg.
Personality
Cheerful, curious and a little stubborn. Loves company and follows its nose everywhere.
Everyday care
Two walks a day, a quick brush each week, and a well-fenced yard. Beagles will happily wander off after a smell.

Golden Retriever

Size
Large. About 51–61 cm tall and 25–34 kg.
Personality
Gentle, patient and eager to please. Great with children and other dogs, and rarely shy.
Everyday care
Long walks or a game of fetch every day, plus brushing two or three times a week to keep the coat tidy.

Poodle

Size
Three sizes: toy, miniature and standard. The standard is about 45–60 cm tall.
Personality
Clever, lively and quick to learn. Poodles enjoy training games and like being near their people.
Everyday care
Daily walks plus play, and a hair cut every six to eight weeks. The curly coat needs brushing often so it does not tangle.
